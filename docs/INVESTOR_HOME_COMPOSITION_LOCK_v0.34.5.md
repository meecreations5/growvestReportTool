# v0.34.5 — Exact Home Reference Lock

The approved Home screenshot supplied by the product owner is the visual source of truth for the phone Investor Home. This pass intentionally stops reinterpreting the composition and keeps the live v0.34.5 data/notification logic inside that approved structure.

## Approved Home composition

1. Full-width Royal Trust Blue wealth hero.
2. Official GrowVest logo at top-left, notification bell and investor profile at top-right.
3. Greeting, Total Wealth, privacy control, previous-update movement and updated time.
4. Compact rounded white quick-action tray overlapping the lower edge of the hero: Portfolio, Bucket List, Reports and Protection.
5. `What needs your attention` previews one highest-priority live item. If more exist, `See all` expands the list in place.
6. Compact `Your Bucket List` goal preview with contextual goal icon, corpus/target, progress and `<1%` handling.
7. Compact Deep Premium Black Monthly Review card with a chart motif and `View Review` action.
8. Compact GrowVest Partner row after the review card.
9. Persistent phone navigation follows the approved reference: Home, Portfolio, GrowVest, Bucket List, Reports.

## Functional behavior retained

- SIP reminders remain data-driven and receive high Home priority when due soon or needing action.
- Protection renewal, portfolio review, Bucket List information and meetings remain part of the attention engine.
- Financial privacy remains app-wide.
- Refresh remains live and icon-only.
- Home movement now exposes the previous-update percentage when a prior verified snapshot exists.
- Reports remains a primary Home quick action even though Profile occupies the fifth persistent phone tab.
