# Signature Branding and Social Media

## Branding Settings

Open **Settings -> Branding -> Signature branding** to configure the company-wide values used by staff signatures:

- Default brand positioning
- Signature website
- Signature office address
- Left and right footer taglines
- LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and X/Twitter URLs
- Global social-media enabled/disabled state

Only social URLs beginning with `http://` or `https://` are published.

Signature logos remain under **Logo & assets** and the Emitha webfont remains under **Colours & typography**.

## Staff Signature

Open **My Signature** or a staff user's signature editor.

- Use **Apply branding defaults** to copy the currently published signature branding into that user's draft.
- Use **Visibility -> Social media profiles** to show or hide the social row for that user.
- Desktop and mobile signatures display compact linked social badges.
- The WhatsApp preview includes the configured social profile URLs as text.

Publish the branding first, then review and publish each affected staff signature after applying the new defaults.
